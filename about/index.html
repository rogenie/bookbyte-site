About BookByte

Clear, accurate guides for people who run their own business and want to handle their own books and taxes.

Who writes the guides

BookByte is written by Rogenie Cordero, founder of Computatio, a bookkeeping and tax service for self-employed owners across the US. Rogenie has 20 years of experience in bookkeeping, tax and finance, a dual degree with a minor in computer science, and more than 20 years of building businesses across industries, both for-profit and nonprofit.

How we check them

Every rate, threshold and deadline is checked against an official source: the IRS or the relevant state tax agency, such as California’s Franchise Tax Board (FTB). Sources are listed at the end of each guide, along with the date it was last reviewed. When a rule changes, the guide is updated.

What BookByte is not

These guides are general information for doing the work yourself. They aren’t tax or legal advice for your situation. If something is unclear or the stakes are high, talk to a tax professional.

If you’d rather not do it yourself

Most people who read these guides can do the work. The usual reason not to is time. Computatio keeps books current for $500 a month and prepares returns at fixed prices, with a person reviewing and signing off on every account.
