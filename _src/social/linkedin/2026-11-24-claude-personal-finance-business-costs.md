Business costs paid on a personal card are easy to forget at tax time.

A software plan here, a domain renewal there. On a personal statement they sit right next to the groceries.

Claude now has a Personal Finance beta (Pro, Max and Ultra plans, US only) that links bank and card accounts through Plaid and reads your transactions. It can't move money. We wrote up a workflow for finding business costs with it. Three takeaways:

1. Run a monthly sweep on a closed month. Ask for every charge that could be a business expense, plus a separate list of recurring subscriptions.
2. Mark each line business, personal or split. The IRS says personal expenses generally aren't deductible, and mixed-use costs must be divided.
3. No receipt, no entry. Check every line against the statement to the cent. Balances in the tool can be a day or two old.

It doesn't replace a separate business account. It does help you catch what slipped through.
