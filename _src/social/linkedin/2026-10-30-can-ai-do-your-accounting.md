An AI chatbot will sort a month of transactions in seconds. The hard part is knowing which answers are wrong, because a wrong answer looks exactly like a right one.

We wrote a guide on what AI gets right in small-business bookkeeping, what it gets wrong, and how to test it on your own books. Three takeaways:

1. AI is strong at pattern work: suggesting categories, reading receipts, matching bank lines and explaining a profit and loss report in plain English.
2. It slips on your business context, current tax rules and long-table math. Example: restaurant meals were 100% deductible only for 2021 and 2022. Meals are generally limited to 50% again, but AI may still quote the old rule.
3. Test it on a month you've already reconciled. Ask it to name every tax rule it used and the tax year, then check each one on the IRS or your state's site.

AI can draft. Your software, your receipts and you still sign off, because you're responsible for what's on your return.
