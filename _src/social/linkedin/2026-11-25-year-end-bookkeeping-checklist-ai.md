We put together a year-end bookkeeping checklist for small-business owners: 7 steps to close 2026, with an AI prompt for each one.

Three things from the guide:

1. Reconcile every account through December 31 first. If your books don't match the statement, nothing after that step is reliable.

2. List the contractors you paid. For payments made in 2026, the IRS threshold for Form 1099-NEC is $2,000 (it was $600), and the form is due to the IRS by January 31, 2027. Card and payment-app payments go on the payment company's 1099-K instead.

3. Let AI draft the lists, then check every number. In our made-up example, the AI counted a refund as a payment and overstated one contractor by $500. The bank statement caught it.

The fourth 2026 estimated tax payment is due January 15, 2027, so clean numbers help there too.
