Groceries on the business card. Software on the personal card. A few months of that, and nobody can tell which charge belongs to the business.

We wrote a guide on using AI to separate business and personal expenses without letting it make the judgment calls. Three takeaways:

1. Give the AI your category list and three to five house rules, then ask it to label every line business, personal, split or unsure, with a reason and no totals.
2. Never accept a business percentage the AI made up. Your car share comes from your mileage log, and your phone and home office shares come from your own records.
3. Personal, household and family costs generally aren't deductible, and mixed-use costs like a car must be divided. A separate business account cuts the split work to a handful of lines a month.

AI does the sorting. You decide the split lines and reconcile each account.
