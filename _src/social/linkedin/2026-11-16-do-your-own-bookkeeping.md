Self-employed and still sorting receipts the week before taxes?

Bookkeeping for a one-person business is simpler than it sounds. It's a record of money in and money out, sorted so you can see your profit. We wrote a plain-English routine that takes about 15 minutes a week and 30 minutes a month.

Three takeaways:

1. Use the expense lines on Schedule C as your categories, plus "owner draw" and "owner contribution." Your books then line up with your return.
2. Reconcile every account to its statement each month. A month that reconciles is a month you can trust.
3. Track profit monthly. Self-employment tax alone is 15.3%, before income tax, so set money aside as you go.
