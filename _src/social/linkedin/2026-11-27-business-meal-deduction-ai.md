That stack of restaurant charges on your card? Half of them might be client meals. You just can't remember which half.

We wrote a guide to sorting business meals with AI without letting the AI guess for you.

Three takeaways:

1. The business meal deduction is generally 50%, and that includes tax and tip. Your books record the full cost; the limit is applied on your return.
2. Entertainment isn't deductible. Food at a game or show counts only if it was bought or billed separately from the tickets.
3. Let AI ask the questions, not answer them. Our prompt pulls every food charge from your statement and asks you who was there and why. You supply the facts, then match each line to a receipt.

The IRS says records made at or near the time carry more weight than notes made later, so we suggest running the prompt once a month instead of once a year.
