QuickBooks now fills in the payee and category on every bank transaction. The risk is hitting Post on all of them without looking.

The new QuickBooks Online banking page marks each AI suggestion with a green, blue or orange signal. We wrote a guide on what each one means and how to review them fast.

Three takeaways:

1. Treat the colors as a review order. Green means a strong pattern in your own history, blue means similar transactions were coded different ways, and orange means little or no history. Scan greens, confirm blues, open every orange.

2. Watch for the "potential match" warning. If a bill or invoice is already in your books, match it. Categorizing it as new counts it twice.

3. Check at month-end. Compare category totals to last month, look for repeat charges, then reconcile each account. Green signals copy your past habits, including the wrong ones.

The guide includes the step-by-step method and a sample review on made-up data.
