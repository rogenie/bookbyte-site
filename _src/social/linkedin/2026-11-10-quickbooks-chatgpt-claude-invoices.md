You asked a chatbot to invoice a client. It did, and that invoice is already in your books.

Intuit says invoicing through QuickBooks in ChatGPT and Claude is now generally available for US QuickBooks customers on Simple Start through Advanced. It's fast. It also writes straight into QuickBooks, so a wrong customer, rate or due date becomes a real invoice and real income on your reports.

We wrote a short guide on using it without the cleanup. Three takeaways:

1. Give the chatbot everything: the exact customer name, each line, quantity, rate and terms. Tell it to stop and ask if anything doesn't match an existing record.
2. Open the invoice in QuickBooks, not just the chat. Compare it line by line to your estimate or contract before you let it send.
3. Tie any number it quotes, like last month's profit, back to the report in QuickBooks.

The guide includes the prompt, a made-up example with three mistakes caught, a checklist, and how to disconnect.
