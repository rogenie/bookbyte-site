AI will sort a month of bank transactions in seconds, and it will put a warehouse-store run in the wrong category with total confidence.

That's the trade-off most owners hit when they try AI for their books. We wrote a practical guide to getting the speed without the errors.

Three takeaways:

1. Give the AI your exact category list and a few rules, and ask for a confidence level and a one-line reason for every transaction. Review everything marked low and anything over an amount you pick.

2. Don't let AI do the totals or replace reconciling. Your books still have to match your bank statement, and the totals should come from your bookkeeping software, not a chat answer.

3. Keep sensitive details out. Strip full account numbers before you upload anything, check whether the tool can train on your data, and turn that off where you can.

The guide includes a prompt you can copy and a sample run on made-up data.
