You exported last month's transactions into a spreadsheet. You still can't say what you spent on what.

ChatGPT now works inside Google Sheets and Excel as an add-on, and OpenAI says it's available on all ChatGPT plans. We wrote a step-by-step guide to using it for one job: a monthly income and expense summary built with real formulas.

Three takeaways:

1. Start from clean data. Use an export from your books or a reconciled bank download, and strip out account numbers and client names before you open the sidebar.
2. Ask for formulas, not typed numbers. The prompt in the guide builds a summary tab with SUMIFS by category, plus a check cell.
3. Check that the summary ties to the cent. In our made-up example, one category label with a trailing space hid $79.99 until the check cell caught it.

AI speeds up the sorting. Reconciling, keeping receipts and reviewing the result are still on you, or on us.
