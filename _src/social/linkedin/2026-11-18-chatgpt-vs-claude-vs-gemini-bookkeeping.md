Which AI should do your bookkeeping: ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini? Most reviews test essays and code, not a month of bank transactions.

So we wrote a guide to running the test yourself, in about 20 minutes, on made-up data.

Three takeaways:

1. On price, the three are close. Each has a free plan, and the main paid plans run $19.99 to $20 a month (checked on each vendor's page November 18, 2026).

2. Set privacy before you upload anything. On personal plans, each tool has a setting that controls whether your chats are used for training, and Google says some Gemini chats are read by human reviewers.

3. Score the tools against an answer key, not a feeling. Build a small test file with three traps: a personal charge, a duplicated row and a vague payee. The tool to worry about is the one that's confidently wrong.

Whichever tool you pick, check its totals to the cent and still reconcile to the bank statement. AI gives you a first pass. It doesn't replace your records.
