You run your profit and loss report every month. Do you know what it's telling you?

Most owners we talk to look at the bottom line and close the tab. The trouble is that a P&L can look normal and still be wrong, and for sole proprietors that report becomes Schedule C at tax time.

We wrote a guide on using any AI chat to read it with you. Three takeaways:

1. Reconcile first. AI can explain a report, but if the books don't match the bank, it's just explaining the errors.
2. Ask it what changed and what looks unusual, then check those lines against receipts and statements yourself. In our made-up example, a $1,500 transfer to the owner sat in "office expense," understating profit by $1,500.
3. Redo the math and tie every total back to your software. AI explains; it doesn't verify.
