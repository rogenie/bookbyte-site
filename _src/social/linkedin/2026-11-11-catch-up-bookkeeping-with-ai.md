Six months of bank statements you haven't touched, and the last 2026 estimated payment is due January 15, 2027.

We wrote a step-by-step guide to catching up on bookkeeping with AI, without trusting it blindly. Three takeaways:

1. Go one month at a time, oldest first. Download that month's statements, have AI sort the transactions into your chart of accounts in small batches, and ask it to flag anything it isn't sure about instead of guessing.

2. Reconcile before you move on. Each month's ending balance must match the bank statement. That is where AI's mistakes show up: refunds read as expenses, card payments counted twice, transfers booked as income.

3. Keep the proof. The IRS says you must be able to prove expenses to deduct them, and a bank statement alone doesn't show what a cost was for. Pull receipts and invoices for anything flagged.

The guide includes a copy-paste prompt, a worked example on made-up data, and five checks to run on the output.
