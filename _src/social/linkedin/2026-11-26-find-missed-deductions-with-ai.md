Business costs hide on personal cards, and a cost you never recorded can't lower your tax.

A software plan on your own card. Parking near a client. A phone you use for work and home. These slip past the books all year, then nobody remembers them in January.

We wrote a guide on using AI to find those costs, and on checking each one before it goes near a return. Three takeaways:

1. Give the AI two lists: a year of bank and card transactions, and the expenses already in your books. Ask it to flag what's missing and to ask you one question about each charge, not decide for you.
2. Verify every candidate. It has to be a business cost, you need the receipt, and mixed-use items like a car must be split between business and personal use.
3. Found something from a past year? The IRS refund window for an amended return is generally 3 years after you filed or 2 years after you paid, whichever is later.

AI finds candidates. You confirm every one.
