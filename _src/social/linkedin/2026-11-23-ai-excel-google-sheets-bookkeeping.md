Your bookkeeping spreadsheet gives you a total, and you're not sure you believe it.

That's the most common thing we hear from owners who keep their books in Excel or Google Sheets. The sheet works until a formula quietly breaks or a bank download gets pasted in twice.

AI can help, if you use it the right way. Three takeaways from our new guide:

1. Ask AI for formulas, not answers. A SUMIFS you can read stays in your sheet. A number typed into a chat window can't be checked.
2. Use a keyword rules table. Map payees to categories once, and let the formula sort most rows. Anything unmatched gets flagged "Review" for a human.
3. Tie out before you trust it. Deposits and withdrawals should match the bank statement, and the summary should equal the sum of every row.

The IRS lets you use any recordkeeping system that clearly shows income and expenses, and a spreadsheet can qualify. Receipts and a bank tie-out are what make it hold up.
