Your P&L looks fine. That doesn't mean it's right.

A charge imported twice, or a transfer to yourself filed as "office expense," hides inside a total until profit looks strange and nobody can say why.

QuickBooks Online Plus and Advanced can now flag these for you. Open the P&L or Balance Sheet, select the Insights icon, then Analyze Books with Accounting AI. It points to possible duplicates, miscategorized items, aging receivables and payables, and income that may need deferring.

We wrote up how we'd use it before month-end:

1. Treat every flag as a question. Open the transaction and match it to the receipt, invoice or bank line before you change anything.
2. Fix with a dated note, so you know later why the number moved.
3. Reconcile to the statement anyway. Intuit says the review doesn't cover every account, so no flags doesn't mean no errors.
