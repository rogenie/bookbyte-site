Your bank export looks tidy. Then your SUMIFS skip rows and your reconciliation is off by a week.

The usual culprits are hidden: a trailing space in a payee name, dates stored as text, "$1,200.00" typed in as text, and duplicate rows from two downloads that overlap.

Copilot in Excel now has a built-in clean-data skill that finds these problems and lets you pick which fixes to apply. Microsoft says it is still rolling out, so it may not be in your Excel yet.

We wrote a short guide on using it safely. Three takeaways:

1. Paste the raw export on its own sheet first, and write down the row count and total deposits and withdrawals.
2. Accept fixes one type at a time: spaces, then dates, then amounts. Leave duplicates for last.
3. Check every suspected duplicate against the bank statement. Two identical charges on one day can both be real.

Then confirm the cleaned totals and ending balance still tie to the statement.
