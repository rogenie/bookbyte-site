Your profit and loss says the business is fine. Your bank balance in six weeks might not agree.

A late client payment, a slower month and the January 15 estimated tax payments can all land in the same stretch. Most owners only find out when the account runs thin.

We wrote a guide to building a cash flow what-if tool with Google Sheets canvas, the Gemini feature that turns a sheet into an interactive mini-app. Three takeaways:

1. Start from your reconciled bank balance, not the number in your banking app. A wrong opening balance makes every week after it wrong.
2. Put 13 weeks of cash in and out on one tab, with assumption cells at the top. Ask canvas for sliders that change only those cells.
3. Before you trust it, set every slider to zero and check that the canvas matches your sheet week by week. Canvas writes back to the sheet, so work on a copy.
