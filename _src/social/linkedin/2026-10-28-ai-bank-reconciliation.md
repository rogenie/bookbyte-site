Your bank balance and your books are $43 apart, and an hour of scrolling hasn't found why.

AI can help with bank reconciliation, but only with the right job. We wrote a guide on using it as a matching assistant, not a calculator.

Three takeaways:

1. Ask AI to match, never to total. Paste your statement and your book entries for the same month, and have it pair each line and list what's left over. Matching is pattern work. Arithmetic across long tables is where AI slips.

2. Sort every difference into normal or error. A bank fee not yet recorded, a deposit after the statement date and a check that hasn't cleared are normal. A missing entry, a duplicate or a wrong amount is an error to fix.

3. Let your software sign off. Count the rows, check the unsure lines with receipts in hand, then reconcile in your bookkeeping software. It's done when the adjusted balances agree there, not when a chat says so.

The guide includes two copy-paste prompts and a sample run on made-up data.
