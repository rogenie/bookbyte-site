Your bank export has your full account number in it. Do you check before you paste it into an AI chat?

AI is good at sorting transactions. It almost never needs the details that make financial data dangerous. Our new guide walks through what to strip out first.

Three takeaways:

1. Share the minimum. To categorize transactions, AI needs the date, description and amount. Delete account numbers, balances and check numbers before you upload.

2. Swap names for labels. Replace client and contractor names with "Client A" or "Contractor B," and keep the key in your own files.

3. Lock the accounts around it. The IRS recommends strong, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication wherever it's offered. You can also get an IRS Identity Protection PIN, a six-digit number that blocks anyone else from filing a federal return with your Social Security number.

The guide includes a copy-paste prompt that scans a file for sensitive details before the AI does any other work, plus a worked example on made-up data.
