Half your spending sits in an account called "Other." That's a chart of accounts problem.

Your chart of accounts is the list of buckets your books sort every dollar into. When the buckets are vague, every report built on them is vague too, and tax time means re-sorting a year of expenses by hand.

We wrote a guide to building one with AI, with a free template. Three takeaways:

1. Tie every expense account to a Schedule C line. Then your year-end profit and loss already reads like your return.
2. Keep meals apart from travel. Most business meals are only 50% deductible, and they go on their own line.
3. Ask AI to flag what it's unsure of instead of guessing. A laptop, a coworking desk or a phone bill can each go more than one way, and your records decide.

AI drafts the list. You trim it, check the line numbers against the form, and reconcile every month to keep it honest.
