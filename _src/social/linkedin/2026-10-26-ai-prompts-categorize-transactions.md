Ask an AI to "categorize these" with no list, and it invents categories like "Food & Dining" that don't match a single line on your tax return.

We see this trip up owners who try AI for their books. So we wrote a library of five copy-and-paste prompts that fix it.

Three takeaways:

1. Give the AI your exact category list first. For a sole proprietor, that's the Schedule C expense lines plus owner draw, owner contribution and transfers. Tell it never to invent a new one.

2. Make it show its work. Ask for a confidence level and a one-line reason on every transaction, and have it mark restaurants, big-box stores, payments to people and transfers as low. You review that short list, not the whole month.

3. Never take totals from the chat. Count rows in and out, match receipts for the lines you reviewed, then post and reconcile in your bookkeeping software.

The guide includes all five prompts and a sample run on made-up data.
