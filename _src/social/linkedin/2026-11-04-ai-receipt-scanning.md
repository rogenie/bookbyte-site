Your bank statement shows you paid. It doesn't show what you bought, and the IRS says proof of payment alone isn't enough to support a deduction.

We wrote a guide on using AI to turn receipt photos into bookkeeping records you can match to your bank lines. Three takeaways:

1. Snap each receipt the same day and save the image in one folder per year. The image is your record. The AI table is only a summary.
2. Tell the AI to write "UNCLEAR" instead of guessing. Otherwise a smudged total gets filled with a number that looks right.
3. Count photos in and rows out, then tie every total to a bank or card line. For travel, gifts and car costs, the IRS generally requires receipts for all lodging and for any other expense of $75 or more.

AI saves the typing. Keeping legible receipts, reconciling and signing off on your return stay with you.
