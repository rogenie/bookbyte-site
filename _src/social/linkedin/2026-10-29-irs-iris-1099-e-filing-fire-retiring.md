If you e-file your contractors' 1099s through the IRS FIRE system, your login won't work for your 2026 forms.

The IRS is retiring FIRE. November 19, 2026, at 3 p.m. Eastern is the last day to file through it. Tax year 2026 Forms 1099 go through IRIS, the IRS's newer system, in the 2027 filing season.

We wrote a guide on making the switch before January. Three takeaways:

1. Apply for an IRIS Transmitter Control Code now. It's separate from your FIRE code, and the IRS says to allow up to 45 calendar days for processing.
2. The IRIS Taxpayer Portal is free. You can type forms in or upload the IRS's own CSV template.
3. AI can total a year of vendor payments and flag who needs a 1099-NEC (the 2026 threshold is $2,000). Mask every taxpayer ID before you paste anything, and tie each total back to your books.

Form 1099-NEC for 2026 is due February 1, 2027, because January 31 falls on a Sunday.
