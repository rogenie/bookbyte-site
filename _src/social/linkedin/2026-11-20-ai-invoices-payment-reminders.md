Writing the third "just following up" email to a client is nobody's favorite part of running a business.

Late payments hurt more than they look. Most self-employed people use the cash method, so an unpaid invoice is income you haven't received yet. And if it never arrives, the IRS generally doesn't let a cash-method business deduct it as a bad debt, because it was never counted as income.

We wrote a short guide on using AI for invoices and payment reminders. Three takeaways:

1. Ask AI to turn your rough notes into clear invoice lines, and tell it not to change any number.
2. Draft a four-email reminder sequence once (before the due date, on it, 7 days late, 21 days late), in your own voice and without fees or threats your contract doesn't include.
3. Run a weekly unpaid list that counts days late from the due date, and mark an invoice paid only when the deposit shows in your bank.
