AI for invoices and payment reminders: prompts that work

You finished the work. Now you’re chasing the money, and writing the third “just following up” email feels awful.

By: Computatio · Last reviewed 2026-11-20

Quick answer. AI can draft clear invoice line items, write a polite reminder sequence (before the due date, on it, and after), and turn your invoice list into a weekly unpaid report. Give it made-up or stripped data, set the tone and dates yourself, and mark an invoice paid only when the money shows up in your bank account.

Your invoices go out late, or they’re unclear

You bill when you remember. The line says “Services, October.” The client’s office has to email back to ask what it covers, and the clock stops.

A vague invoice is easy to set aside.

And asking to be paid feels awkward

You don’t want to sound pushy with a client you like. So you wait. One week late becomes four.

What late payments cost you

  • Cash you counted on. Most self-employed people use the cash method. The IRS says that means you report income in the year you receive it. An unpaid invoice is money you can’t spend yet.
  • No tax write-off if it never comes. The IRS says a cash-method taxpayer generally can’t take a bad debt deduction for unpaid fees. A business bad debt is deductible only if the amount was already included in your income. For most freelancers, an unpaid invoice was never income, so there’s nothing to deduct.
  • Records that don’t match. The IRS can generally assess more tax for 3 years after you file. Your invoices and deposits should tell the same story.

Picture this instead

Invoices go out the day the work is done, with lines the client understands. Reminders go out on a schedule, in your voice. Every Friday you see one short list: who owes what, and how late.

The 3-prompt method for AI invoices and payment reminders

These prompts work in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot. Before you paste anything, remove bank details, tax ID numbers and client contact details. Our guide to keeping financial data safe with AI lists what to strip.

1. Write invoice lines a client can approve

Prompt to copy:
Rewrite these notes as invoice line items for a client. One line per deliverable, with a short plain description, quantity, rate and amount. Do not change any number. Add a total and check that it equals the sum of the lines. Notes: [paste your notes].

2. Draft a reminder sequence in your voice

Prompt to copy:
Write four short payment reminder emails for invoice [number], [amount], due [date]. Email 1: three days before the due date, friendly. Email 2: on the due date. Email 3: seven days late, direct but polite. Email 4: 21 days late, firm, and asks for a payment date. Keep each under 90 words. Restate the invoice number, amount and due date in each one. Don’t threaten, and don’t mention fees or legal action unless I tell you they’re in my contract.

3. Turn your invoice list into an unpaid report

Prompt to copy:
From the attached invoice list (Invoice, Client, Date sent, Due date, Amount, Date paid), list every unpaid invoice as of [today’s date]. Group them as not yet due, 1–30 days late, 31–60 days late, and over 60 days late. Show a total for each group and a grand total. Show your math. Don’t mark anything paid that has no Date paid.
Example (made-up data). A freelance copywriter, report run November 20, 2026.
Invoices: #101 Client A $1,200, due Oct 15, paid Oct 14 · #102 Client B $850, due Oct 31, unpaid · #103 Client C $2,000, due Nov 10, unpaid · #104 Client A $600, due Dec 5, unpaid.
Report: not yet due $600 (#104); 1–30 days late $2,850 (#102 at 20 days, #103 at 10 days). Grand total $3,450.
What went wrong on the first run: the AI put #102 in “31–60 days” by counting from the date sent. Fixed by telling it to count days late from the due date.

How to check AI invoices and reminders before they go out

  1. Numbers: every amount, date and invoice number matches your invoice, to the cent.
  2. Totals: add the lines yourself or with a SUM formula.
  3. Paid status: match each “paid” invoice to a deposit. Our AI bank reconciliation guide shows how.
  4. Tone: read each reminder out loud. Would you send it to this client?
  5. Promises: cut any late fee, discount or deadline the AI added that isn’t in your contract.

Can AI send invoices and reminders for me?

A chat tool drafts; it doesn’t track who paid. Many invoicing apps can send scheduled reminders, and you can paste your AI-written text into them. Either way, you stay in charge of what gets sent and what counts as paid.

Keep a copy of every invoice you send. It backs up the income on your return and the deposits in your bank. When payments arrive, sort them with our AI prompts to categorize transactions.
