Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026.

What we collect

BookByte has no accounts, forms or comments, and doesn’t set its own cookies. We don’t sell or share personal information.

Newsletter

If you sign up for the free write-offs guide, your email address is collected by Kit, the service that runs the Computatio newsletter, under its own privacy policy, and by Computatio under the Computatio privacy policy. You can unsubscribe from any email.

Services that load with the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and basic browser information when you load a page, under its own privacy policy. Fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Visit counts

We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. It sets no cookies and doesn’t track you across sites. It records the page you visited, the site that sent you, and general details such as browser, screen size and country. It doesn’t store your IP address.

No ad pixels or tracking cookies

BookByte doesn’t use advertising pixels (such as Meta, Google, LinkedIn or TikTok), session recording or chat widgets, and doesn’t let third parties collect information about your activity across other websites through this site. The share buttons are plain links; nothing is sent to a social network unless you click one.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we don’t track you across websites or sell or share personal information, there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off. We treat either signal as a request not to sell or share your information, which we already don’t do.

California residents

We don’t sell or share personal information, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act. To ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, use the contact details below.

Changes

If we add another analytics service, advertising tool or form, this page will be updated first, with a new date, and will say what is collected and why. Where the law requires it, we’ll ask for your consent first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through computatio.io.
